Values conversations

Two guided conversations for a couple, done together on one phone, tablet or laptop. Neither gives a score.

Values DeckSort value cards privately, then compare them together. About 90 minutes. Enough of BothTalk about security and novelty, then agree small experiments with a review date. Two sittings.

Answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere: the pages block all network requests. You can save your progress, or a PDF report, as files on your own device.

These are conversation guides, not therapy or assessments.
